01 Research

Designing tactile information for more accessible interaction.

My PhD research explores how people perceive and navigate tactile patterns. I investigate the perceptual relationships between raised-line graphics and the transitions between them, with the aim of making tactile information clearer and more meaningful.

I contribute to research on automatic tactile-map generation, combining geometric tactile patterns with multisensory analysis to help people with visual impairments perceive and interact with the physical world.
