Research

Making information
feel more accessible.

My PhD research explores how people perceive and navigate tactile patterns. I study the relationship between raised-line graphics and the transitions between them, with the goal of making tactile information clearer and more meaningful.

01

Tactile graphics

Designing raised-line patterns that communicate visual and spatial information through touch.

02

Haptic perception

Understanding how people distinguish tactile patterns and sense transitions during exploration.

03

Inclusive interaction

Building multisensory tools and methods with people with visual impairments in mind.
